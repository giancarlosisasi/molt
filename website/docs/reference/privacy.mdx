# Privacy

This page covers the documentation site. The `molt` command-line tool collects nothing.

## Analytics

The site counts page views with Google Analytics 4. Google is contacted only after you accept.
Reject, or answer nothing, and no request is made and no cookie is set.

Your answer stays in this browser under `molt:analytics-consent`. Clearing your site data removes
it.

| Collected | Detail |
|---|---|
| Page | Address and title, and the page that linked you here |
| Device | Browser, operating system, screen size, and language |
| Place | Country and region. Google reads these from your IP address, then drops it |
| Activity | Page views, scroll depth, outbound clicks, and site searches |

Advertising is off for every visitor. Ad storage, ad personalization, and ad data sharing are set
to `denied`, so no advertising cookie is written.

## Cookies

Two, both from Google, both written only after you accept.

| Cookie | Purpose | Lifetime |
|---|---|---|
| `_ga` | Tells one browser from another | 2 years |
| `_ga_WK4TPRME0B` | Holds this site's session state | 2 years |

Nothing else is stored, and no cookie follows you to another site.
